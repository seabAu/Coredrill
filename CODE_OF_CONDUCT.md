# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Project participation must be respectful, patient, and constructive. Welcome differing experience levels and perspectives; critique ideas and code rather than people; protect privacy; and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, or publication of another person's private information.

Job searching is unusually sensitive. Never pressure someone to disclose career, identity, medical, demographic, compensation, immigration, or contact information to participate in this project.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, project communication, research sessions, and public representation of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an established private channel. Do not include sensitive career data in a report. The owner may warn, limit, or remove participation when behavior threatens safety or constructive collaboration. Conflicts involving the owner require an independent trusted reviewer before a public contribution community is opened.

Enforcement procedures and contact routes will be expanded before public contributions begin.
