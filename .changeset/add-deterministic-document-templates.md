Add deterministic, AI-disabled cover-letter and application-answer templates over explicitly selected reviewed evidence. Outputs use canonical Coredrill Document IR, retain source-version context, map every section to job context, selected evidence, or style-only text, and fail closed for private, unreviewed, missing, or non-draftable inputs. The application boundary derives answer eligibility from the existing versioned question classifier and never calls a renderer for sensitive or manual-only categories.
